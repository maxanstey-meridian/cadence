# Lathe Prompt Parity Matrix

## Scope

This matrix is the final source-based comparison of Lathe's single-run prompt
contract against Cadence. Evidence points to the implementations and behavioral
tests inspected on 2026-08-11. Lathe campaign, queue, daemon, cross-run, and model
promotion mechanics are outside Cadence's declared single-run scope.

## Remaining Gap

Tandem can prove successful tool-name observations, including generated
`run_verification_N` commands. It cannot prove exact tool arguments, exact
base/candidate values, complete pagination, inspection of every returned path,
semantic use, failed invocation output, or the existence of typed file-line/symbol
references. Those clauses remain mandatory and prompt-enforced.
